# Privacy Policy for CLC Photography

> Effective Date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
> Website: clcphotos.com

## 1. Who We Are

CLC Photography (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides youth sports photography services and an online ordering platform for photo packages and digital downloads.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to process orders and deliver photos.

Information you provide:

- Parent/guardian name
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)
- Player information (name, team, school/organization, jersey number, etc.)
- Order details (packages, items, personalization)
- Notes or special instructions

### Automatically collected:

- Device/browser information
- IP address
- Basic usage data for performance and debugging

### Payment information:

- Payments are processed by third parties such as Square, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App
- We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly to:

- Process and manage photo orders
- Link orders to photo sessions using QR codes and/or NFC tags
- Deliver digital downloads and order confirmations
- Communicate about orders (receipts, updates, issues)
- Provide customer support
- Improve system performance and reliability
- Request consent for optional uses such as marketing or promotional materials (only when applicable)
- We do not sell personal information.

### Text Messages (SMS)

If you sign up for text messages, we use your mobile number to send messages about your orders, payments, and photo day, and to reply to you. We will not sell or share your mobile phone number or related mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. We share it with service providers only as needed to operate our services and deliver messages. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Full terms: clcphotos.com/sms-terms.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Our services involve youth sports participants, but purchases are intended to be made by parents, guardians, or authorized adults.

We handle information related to minors solely for:

- Identifying players for photo organization
- Fulfilling photo orders
- We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental involvement.
- We do not use children’s data for advertising or profiling
- We do not use images of minors for marketing or promotional purposes without verifiable parental or guardian consent obtained separately

## 5. Photo Handling

- Photos are taken during organized events (schools, leagues, teams)
- Images are linked to orders using our internal ticketing system (QR codes and/or NFC tags)
- Access to images may be restricted (e.g., by ticket, email, or order lookup)
- Photos are used for order fulfillment only by default

### Marketing and Promotional Use

If your organization (school/league) has its own media or consent policies, those may also apply.

- We do not use photos for marketing or promotional purposes without explicit permission
- If we would like to use a photo for marketing, we will contact the parent or guardian associated with the order
- A separate photo release/consent form will be provided at that time
- No marketing use will occur unless consent is granted

## 6. How Information Is Shared

We only share data when necessary to operate the service:

- Payment processors (e.g., Square, PayPal)
- Hosting and infrastructure providers
- Email delivery services for receipts and notifications
- Text message (SMS) delivery providers, only to deliver texts you have signed up for
- These providers receive only the data required to perform their function.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to:

- Fulfill orders
- Provide access to purchased photos
- Maintain business and tax records
- After that, data may be deleted or anonymized.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, including:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Secure payment processing via third-party providers
- Access controls for administrative systems
- No system is completely secure, but we take appropriate precautions.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Request access to your data
- Request correction or deletion
- Opt out of certain communications
- To make a request, contact: privacy@info.clcphotos.com

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

We may use minimal cookies or similar technologies to:

- Maintain sessions
- Improve performance
- Monitor basic usage
- We do not sell or use behavioral tracking data for advertising.

## 11. Third-Party Links

Our site or communications may include links to third-party services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
